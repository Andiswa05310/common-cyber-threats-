# common-cyber-threats-
a threat is a possibility that something bad could happen.
vulnerability is a weakness in a system that can be exploited by threat.
An attack is when a threat is carried out.
phishing is the type of social engineering attack where attackers send fake emails or websites to trick users into giving away personal information like passwords 
example: fake email from "UNIZULU IT Support" asking students to click a link and "verify their account" or lose access.
how to protect myself:Check the sender’s email address carefully
- Don’t click suspicious links
- Enable MFA on your accounts
- Report suspicious emails
Malware it's any software designed to harm, steal data or disrupting computers systeo
example: Downloading a "free game" that secretly installs spyware to record your keystrokes.
way to protect a user: Install and update antivirus software
- Only download software from trusted sources
- Keep your OS and apps updated
- Don’t open attachments from unknown senders
ransomware A type of malware that encrypts/locks all files on a computer and demands payment, usually in crypto, to give you the decryption key.
example: an attack that locks hospitals' computers and demand bitcoin to unlock them
ways to protect users: Regularly back up your files to cloud/external drive
- Don’t open suspicious email attachments
- Keep software patched
- Use endpoint security/antivirus
social engineering, manipulating people to trick them into giving away confidential information 
example:someone calls pretending to be bank staff asking for you OTP to access your account 
ways to protect the user: Verify identity before giving info
- Never share passwords or OTPs over call/chat
- Be skeptical of "urgent" requests
- Train staff/students on awareness
password attacks,attacks steal passwords 
example:hackers using leaked passwords to try login into people's accounts
ways to protect user: - Use strong, unique passwords
- Use a password manager
- Enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
- Change passwords regular
DOS attack,one computer floods a server to make it crash
example: Hackers flooding a university registration website during enrollment so no one can register
ways to protect user: - Use firewalls and DDoS protection services like Cloudflare
- Rate limiting on servers
- Monitor network traffic for spikes
- Have backup servers/load balancers
